26 Sycamore Grove is a most attractive three-bedroom home, ideally positioned in a quiet cul-de-sac within the highly sought-after Westminster Park development. Presented to the market by Hunters Estate Agents, this superb property extends to approximately 98 sq.m. (1,055 sq.ft.), with an additional attic space of approximately 19 sq.m. (205 sq.ft.).

The property offers bright, generously proportioned accommodation throughout, complemented by a beautifully landscaped, exceptionally private and sunny rear garden. Tastefully upgraded and extended by the current owners, the home benefits from a host of appealing features, including an extended kitchen and living space, a converted attic room with staircase access, new windows and doors, bespoke wall panelling, and upgraded kitchen and bathroom facilities.

On entering, a welcoming hallway leads to the well-appointed living room and guest WC. The living room features a wall-mounted Gasco fire and double doors opening into the impressive open-plan kitchen/dining/family area. The modernised kitchen is centred around a stylish breakfast bar with quartz worktop and flows seamlessly into the dining and separate family seating areas. With direct access to the rear garden, this versatile space is perfectly suited to both everyday family living and entertaining. Upstairs, there are three bedrooms, including two generous double bedrooms and a single bedroom currently arranged as a dressing room. A spacious family bathroom completes the first-floor accommodation. Rising to the second floor is a large attic room, featuring skylight windows and excellent eaves storage, providing a versatile multi-functional additional space.

To the front, a paved driveway provides valuable off-street parking. The rear garden is a particular highlight, having been thoughtfully landscaped to incorporate a paved patio, decking and gravelled areas. Exceptionally private and enjoying excellent sunshine, it provides an ideal setting for outdoor dining, entertaining and relaxation during the warmer months. A high-quality steel shed also provides excellent additional storage.

Sycamore Grove is a mature and highly regarded residential development, conveniently located close to a wide range of local amenities. The nearby villages of Foxrock, Blackrock, Deansgrange and Stillorgan offer an excellent selection of shops, cafés, restaurants and leisure facilities. The area is also particularly well served by a wide choice of sporting clubs catering for rugby, football, hockey, GAA and more.

The property is exceptionally well positioned for access to a number of highly regarded schools. Loreto College Foxrock is just moments away, while Hollypark National School and St Brigid’s National School, Foxrock, are within easy walking distance. Other nearby schools include Clonkeen College, Blackrock College, CBC Monkstown, Mount Anville Secondary School and Sion Hill.

The area also benefits from excellent transport connections. The nearby N11 Quality Bus Corridor provides access to a range of Dublin Bus routes, while the M50 is within convenient reach. The LUAS Green Line at Carrickmines is also readily accessible, providing a direct connection to Dublin City Centre and beyond.

Viewing is highly recommended.
