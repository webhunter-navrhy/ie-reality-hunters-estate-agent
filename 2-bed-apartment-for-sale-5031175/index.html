Hunters Estate Agents are delighted to present to the market this excellent two-bedroom property, extending to approximately 55 sq. m. / 592 sq. ft.

No. 11 Monkstown Square is an attractive own-door, ground-floor apartment, ideally situated within a quiet and secure development of just 28 apartments, located just off Monkstown Avenue. This charming property also benefits from rear access to the beautifully maintained communal gardens.

The property is approached through a bright entrance hallway, which provides access to all principal rooms. The kitchen is positioned to the front and features convenient breakfast-bar style seating. To the rear, the spacious and light-filled living room enjoys a pleasant outlook over the patio and landscaped communal gardens, with a feature open fireplace creating a warm and inviting focal point. There are two well-proportioned bedrooms and a family bathroom, complete with bath and electric shower.

Monkstown Square is a secure, gated development enjoying an enviable location close to Monkstown, Blackrock and Deansgrange villages. Residents have an excellent choice of amenities nearby, including shopping centres, specialist retailers, restaurants, cafés and a wide selection of local services. The area is also renowned for its excellent choice of schools, creches and third-level educational institutions.

The property is exceptionally well served by public transport, with numerous bus routes nearby and the DART station at Monkstown providing convenient access to Dublin city centre and beyond. The N11 and M50 are also within easy reach, offering excellent connectivity throughout Dublin and the surrounding areas.

This is an appealing property in a highly sought-after location and would make an ideal home or investment opportunity.

Viewing is highly recommended.
